If you used Web-CDI to collect data, cite the platform paper:

deMayo, B., Kellier, D., Braginsky, M., Bergmann, C., Hendriks, C., Rowland, C. F., Frank, M. C., & Marchman, V. A. (2021). Web-CDI: A system for online administration of the MacArthur-Bates Communicative Development Inventories. Language Development Research, 1(1), 55–98. https://doi.org/10.34842/kr8e-w591

If you used the CDI-CAT, also cite the CAT paper:

Kachergis, G., Marchman, V. A., Dale, P. S., Mankewitz, J., & Frank, M. C. (2022). Online computerized adaptive tests of children's vocabulary development in English and Mexican Spanish. Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research, 65(6), 2288–2308. https://doi.org/10.1044/2022_JSLHR-21-00372

For the CDI instruments themselves, cite the manual:

Marchman, V. A., Dale, P. S., & Fenson, L. (2023). MacArthur-Bates Communicative Development Inventories: User's Guide and Technical Manual (3rd ed.). Brookes Publishing. brookespublishing.com/product/cdi
