What's new in Web-CDI

In 2026 we moved Web-CDI to new hosting and worked through a long list of bugs. Here is what changes for you. Questions? Write to {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}.

What stays the same

  • Your account, groups, participants and their responses came with you.
  • The site address is the same, and links you have already sent to parents still work.
  • You may need to log in again once.

Scores and percentiles

We recalculated the scores of all existing administrations. Most percentiles are unchanged or differ slightly, by 1 or 2 points, from a file you downloaded earlier. Keep your earlier files if you need to reproduce an analysis you already ran.

Which forms change:

  • Words & Sentences (English, Canadian English and Spanish): now use the same norms as Wordbank, extended to 36 months. A few values at each age differ by 1 point, rarely 2. Children aged 31 to 36 months were compared with 30-month norms before and are now compared with children their own age, so their percentiles can change by more.
  • English CDI-III, English Level I and Level II (A and B), French (French) CAT and Japanese CAT: their published tables give only every fifth percentile. These are now filled in by straight-line interpolation, and percentiles rose by about 2 points on average.
  • All CAT forms: the summary scores were corrected (the ability estimate was stored as 0), so CAT percentiles and estimated words produced can differ considerably.
  • Words & Gestures (English, Canadian English and Spanish), English CAT norms and Spanish CAT norms: the tables are unchanged and used exactly as published. The rules below can still change individual values.

Rules for every form:

  • Scores below the 1st percentile are reported as “<1”, and the top of the scale is 99. When several percentiles share the same raw score, the highest applies.
  • Sex-specific percentiles are marked “not computed” when a child's sex is recorded as something other than male or female.
  • Prematurity adjustment applies only when the parent said the child was born early or late and gave the number of weeks.

Changes that may affect how you work

  • Participant IDs with leading zeros (such as 007) are now refused when you add them, because the zeros were being dropped without warning.
  • The fixed-width zip export for the old CDI scoring program has been removed. It was already off the Download menu.
  • New registrations can't reuse an email address that already has an account.
  • Background columns in data downloads now match what your group's form actually collects, so a file may have fewer columns than before.
  • Deleted administrations are left out of downloads, clinical reports and the API, and their participant links stop working. Deleted administrations still count toward numbering, so numbers aren't reused.
  • Deleting a group now closes its participant links too. Deleting never removes your data: if you delete an administration or a group by mistake, write to webcdi-contact@stanford.edu and we can restore it, with its links and answers.
  • Turning off a group's reusable link now closes it fully: new participants can't start through it.
  • On groups that skip the demographic form, an external platform's link reopens an existing administration only if it carries the same sex and an age within one month of the stored age.

Improvements for researchers

  • Log in with your username or your email address.
  • Group search now finds partial matches, keeps your search term and works with the Enter key.
  • Deleting administrations works again, and counts on the dashboard and group pages leave out deleted ones.
  • Uploading a file of participant IDs, or editing an ID to one already in use, now gives a clear message instead of hanging.
  • Downloads and clinical reports are more reliable: several crashes and blank pages are fixed, and selecting only unfinished forms now explains why there's nothing to download.
  • Participant links can be downloaded for every form, including CAT.
  • English CDI3R can now be selected on the Instruments page, with no Brookes code needed.
  • Dates in tables are unambiguous (Nov 2, 2026), and status labels are easier to tell apart.
  • Studies that receive completion notices get one per completed form, with automatic retries if their server doesn't respond.

Improvements for parents

  • Saving and coming back returns parents to the section they were on, and they aren't asked to consent a second time.
  • Pages load faster, especially the results page after finishing a form.
  • “Show my answers” shows parents the answers they gave again, on a read-only panel.
  • Clearer buttons to show or hide the results graph and answers.
  • Corrected typos and question layout on several forms.

Under the hood

  • Up-to-date software and security patches, with stricter checks on who can see and change what.
  • Automated testing and deployment, monitoring, and regular backups.
