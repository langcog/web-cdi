About Web-CDI

What is Web-CDI?

Web-CDI is the online administration system for the MacArthur-Bates Communicative Development Inventories (CDIs), parent-report instruments for measuring young children's early language development. The system is supported by the CDI Advisory Board and housed at Stanford University.

All data stored in Web-CDI are de-identified: no information about individual respondents' identities is stored in the database, including child date of birth. Users are strongly encouraged to share their de-identified data with Wordbank, an open database of CDI administrations across many languages.

Citing Web-CDI

See deMayo et al. (2021) Language Development Research for more information about Web-CDI, and Kachergis et al. (2022) Journal of Speech, Language & Hearing Research for the CDI-CAT. More about the English CDI instruments is in the MacArthur-Bates User's Guide and Technical Manual, 3rd Edition (Marchman, Dale, & Fenson, 2023).
