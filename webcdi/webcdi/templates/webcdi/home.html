About Web-CDI

Web-CDI is an online platform for administering MB-CDI instruments (long and short forms) in several different languages. Web-CDI is sponsored by the MacArthur-Bates Communicative Development Inventory (CDI) Advisory Board and is completely free to register for an account. Once you login to your account, navigate to the Instruments tab to see the languages and form types that are available. Note that the North American English long forms (American/Canadian English WG and WS), Mexican Spanish long forms (WG and WS), American English CDI III, and American English/Mexican Spanish CDI-CAT are distributed through Brookes Publishing Company and require an access code that can be purchased at brookespublishing.com/webcdi. (If you are having any issues with your code, please contact the Brookes support team.) All other instruments are available free-of-charge.

More information about the American English suite of CDI instruments can be found in the MacArthur-Bates User's Guide and Technical Manual, 3rd Edition (Marchman, Dale, and Fenson, 2023), also available from Brookes Publishing Company. Users of instruments other than the American English may also find the manual handy, especially chapters that review the logic and history of the CDIs, administration and scoring guidelines, and recommendations for using CDIs in clinical and bilingual/multilingual contexts. See deMayo et al. (2021) Language Development Research for more information about the Web-CDI platform and its development.

Having difficulty registering or other technical difficulties? Contact the Web-CDI team at webcdi-contact@stanford.edu. For more information about the MB-CDI itself, see mb-cdi.stanford.edu.
